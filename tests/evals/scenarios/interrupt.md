# Interrupt

## Steps
1. SendNowait: "Write a very long and detailed essay about the entire history of computing from the abacus to modern quantum computers, covering every major milestone"
2. Sleep: 10
3. SendNowait: "/stop"
4. Sleep: 30

## Criteria
- The agent begins responding about computing history
- The output contains "(interrupting...)" indicating the /stop command was received
- The response is truncated or cut short (does not reach quantum computers or a natural conclusion)
- The agent does not produce a complete essay covering all topics from abacus to quantum computing
